How to Use App
How to Use App
Master Togan is a personal mobile app — five pages, one mission. Here is exactly how everything works:

Home
Your landing page. Quick-access boards take you directly into any section of the app. Tap the app title at the top of any page to return here instantly.

Feed
Master Togan's live post feed. Stories appear as a scrollable row at the top — tap any story to watch it full-screen, swipe up or tap the close button to exit. Below the stories, scroll through posts: tap the heart to like a post (one like per post, 60-second cooldown between actions). A dot on the Feed icon means new posts or stories have been added since your last visit.

Quest
Questions, challenges, and frameworks that sharpen your thinking. Tap any card to expand the full answer. Cast a thumbs-up or thumbs-down vote to register your take — one vote per quest, with a cooldown to prevent rapid changes. A dot on the Quest icon means new quests have been posted since your last visit.

Block
The Block page has three tabs — Stories, Updates, and Poll — all in one place.

Stories — A dedicated story viewer. Tap any story card to open it full-screen. Swipe up or tap the back arrow to close.

Updates — Curated recommendations and insights from Master Togan. Tap the filter icon next to the count to browse updates by category (Dating, Money, Frame, or any custom category). Tap any card to read the full breakdown. Some updates include a direct action link — tap it to follow through. A dot on the Block icon means new updates are waiting.

Poll — Live polls from Master Togan. Tap an option to cast your vote — one vote per poll. Results update in real time across all users. Once a poll has ended, the final vote distribution is shown and voting is closed.

Store
Browse Apps, Books, and Circles handpicked by Master Togan. Switch between the three tabs at the top of the page. Tap any item to open its full detail panel — description, links, and more. Use the search bar to find something fast. Tap the back arrow or close button to return to the list. Tap the like button once to show support for any item (one like per item).

Notifications
Tap the bell icon at the top right of any page to see all official announcements, drops, and updates from Master Togan. Tap any notification card to expand the full message.

Profile
Tap your avatar at the top left to visit Master Togan's profile page. You can like his profile once to show support — the like count is shared and visible across all users. Tap any linked content on the profile to open it directly.
