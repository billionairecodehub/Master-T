How to Use App
How to Use App
get In With game is Master Togan's personal mobile app — five pages, one mission. Here is how to move around:

🏠 Home
Your starting point. Tap the app title at the top of any page to return here instantly.

⚔️ Quest
Questions, challenges, and frameworks that sharpen understanding. Tap any card to expand the full answer. Cast a thumbs-up or thumbs-down vote to register your take. A dot on the icon means new quests have been added since your last visit.

🗳️ Polls
Live polls from Master Togan. Tap an option to cast your vote — you get one vote per poll. Results update in real time. Ended polls show the final vote count. A dot on the icon means a new poll has been posted.

📋 Updates
Curated recommendations and updates from Master Togan. Tap any card to read the full breakdown. Some updates include a direct link — tap it to follow through. A dot on the icon means there is new content you have not read yet.

📱 App (this page)
Browse Apps, Books, and Circles handpicked by Master Togan. Switch between the three tabs at the top. Tap any item to open its full detail panel. Use the search bar to find something fast. Tap the back arrow to return to the list.

🔔 Notifications
Tap the bell icon at the top right to see all official announcements and drops. Tap any notification to expand it.

👤 Profile
Tap your avatar at the top left to visit Master Togan's profile. You can like his profile once to show support — the count is shared across all users.
