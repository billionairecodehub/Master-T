How to Use App
How to Use App
Six sections. Everything intentional. Here's how it all works.

Home
Your base. Tap any board to jump straight into a section. Tap the app title at the top of any page to come back here instantly.

Feed
Stories run across the top — tap one to watch full-screen, swipe up or tap close to exit. Below, scroll through posts and tap the heart to like (one like per post, 60-second cooldown). A dot on the Feed icon means something new is waiting.

Quest
Frameworks and challenges that sharpen how you think. Tap any card to read the full answer. Vote thumbs-up or down to register your take — one vote per quest. A dot means new quests are live.

Block
Three tabs in one place — Stories, Updates, and Poll.

Stories — Tap a card to open full-screen. Swipe up or tap the arrow to close.

Updates — Insights and recommendations from Master Togan. Filter by category using the icon next to the count. Tap any card for the full breakdown. A dot means new updates are in.

Poll — Tap an option to vote. One vote per poll, results live for all users. Closed polls show the final count.

Store
Apps, Books, and Circles — curated by Master Togan. Switch tabs at the top, tap any item for its full detail panel. Use the search bar to find fast. Like once to show support.

Master Profile
Tap the avatar top-left for Master Togan's profile. Like once to show support — the count is live across all users. Tap any linked item to open it directly.

Notifications
Tap the bell icon top-right to see all announcements and drops. Tap any card to expand the message.

Your Profile
Your personal account page. Tap your avatar in the header to open it. See your username, email, gender, marital status, and how long your account has been active. Tap the settings icon to edit your photo, username, email, or any profile detail. Tap Log-Out to sign out of the app.
