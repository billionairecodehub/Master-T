How to Use App
How to Use App
Master Togan | get In With game is a single mobile app that brings together content, tools, and community in one place. Here is how each part works: Feed — The Feed is your main content stream. New posts from Master Togan appear here automatically. Tap any post to open and read it in full. You can like a post once and leave an impression. A red dot on the Feed icon means there is new content you have not seen yet. Quest — The Quest page is for Questions challenges frameworks that helps understanding & insight. Tap any quest card to expand it and read the full solution. You can cast a thumbs up or thumbs down vote on each one. A red dot here means a new quest has been added since your last visit. Menu — Browse Apps, Books, and Circles recommended by Master Togan. Switch between the three tabs at the top. Tap any item to open its full detail panel. Use the search bar to find something specific. Tap the back arrow to return to the list. Notifications — All official announcements, updates, and drops from Master Togan are delivered here. Tap a notification to expand it. Profile — The Profile page belongs to Master Togan. You can like his profile once to show support. The like count is shared globally across all users.
